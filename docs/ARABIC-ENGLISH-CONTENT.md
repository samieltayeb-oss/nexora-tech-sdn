## 4. Technical RTL & Bidirectional Formatting Rules

1. **Root Document Attributes:**
   - `/en/*` renders `<html lang="en" dir="ltr">`.
   - `/ar/*` renders `<html lang="ar" dir="rtl">`.
2. **Bidirectional Isolation (`dir="ltr"`):**
   - Phone numbers (`+249 120 4040 45`), email addresses (`r.suliman@nexora-tech.com`), URLs (`www.nexora-tech.com`), and technical serials/hashes are wrapped with `dir="ltr"` and `unicode-bidi: isolate` (`className="ltr-inline"`) so `+` signs and domain segments never invert in Arabic RTL layouts.
3. **Directional Icon & Layout Mirroring:**
   - Directional arrows (`→`) automatically flip via `rtl:rotate-180` in Arabic.
   - Grid flows, timelines, breadcrumbs, and form alignments use logical properties (`ms-*`, `me-*`, `ps-*`, `pe-*`, `start-*`, `end-*`, `text-start`).
4. **ThreeUI `SylvaHero` RTL Isolation:**
   - The ThreeUI `SylvaHero` iframe remains untouched on disk (`inner-green-3d.html`) so its internal WebGL viewport and absolute coordinate stage (`calc(N * var(--u))`) never break, while our isolated `applyScene` adapter injects Arabic typography (`IBM Plex Sans Arabic`) and `dir="rtl"` exclusively on the text containers (`h1.headline`, `p.lede`, `.card--about`, `.card--stove`, `.stat--a`, `.stat--b`) inside the live frame when viewing `/ar`.
